Topple Smasher Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Topple Smasher ("the game") is made by BuildByShahzad ("we"). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Gameplay data such as your level progress, stars, coins and settings. It is stored on your device.
  • Device information and advertising ID, collected by Google AdMob to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This can include approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and crash or diagnostic data.
  • Purchase information, handled by Google Play Billing when you buy something in the game. We never see your payment details.

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To show ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings (Privacy options).
  • To deliver in-app purchases.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.
